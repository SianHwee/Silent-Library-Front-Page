Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

Silent Library ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of our members and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information.

Policy details

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal data when you register for membership, borrow books, register for events, or contact us through this website:

  • Full name, date of birth and contact details (address, phone number, email address)
  • Membership and borrowing history
  • Event registration details
  • Messages submitted through our contact form
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and device, collected automatically for site performance

2. How We Use Your Information

Personal data is used solely to operate library services, including:

  • Managing membership accounts and book loans
  • Sending due-date reminders, event confirmations and newsletters (with consent)
  • Responding to enquiries submitted via the contact form
  • Improving our website and services

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent personal data to third parties. Information may only be shared with trusted service providers who support our library operations (e.g. IT hosting), and only to the extent necessary, or where required by law.

4. Data Retention

Membership records are retained for as long as an account remains active, and for a reasonable period afterwards for administrative and legal purposes, after which data is securely deleted or anonymised.

5. Cookies

This website may use essential cookies to remember your preferences and ensure core functionality. No third-party advertising cookies are used.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at hello@silentlibrary.sg or via our Contact page. We will respond to all requests within a reasonable timeframe in accordance with applicable data protection law.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out via our Contact page.
