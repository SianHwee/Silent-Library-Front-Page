The Hobbit

by J.R.R. Tolkien

4.5 / 5 (289 reader ratings)

Bilbo Baggins wants nothing more than a quiet life and a full pantry, until a wizard and thirteen dwarves arrive to recruit him as a burglar. The Hobbit follows his journey across Middle-earth to the Lonely Mountain and the dragon Smaug.

Written for Tolkien's own children and later the doorway to The Lord of the Rings, it is the title we most often recommend to readers aged ten and upward.
