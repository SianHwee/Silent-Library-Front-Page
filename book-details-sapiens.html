Sapiens

by Yuval Noah Harari

4.5 / 5 (401 reader ratings)

Sapiens traces the history of our species from insignificant African ape to planetary force, organised around three revolutions: the cognitive, the agricultural and the scientific. Harari's central claim is that shared fictions — money, nations, religions, corporations — are what allow humans to cooperate at scale.

Sweeping and deliberately provocative, it is equally popular with sixth-form history students and with our adult reading circle.
