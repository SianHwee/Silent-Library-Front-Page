To Kill a Mockingbird

by Harper Lee

5.0 / 5 (356 reader ratings)

Narrated by six-year-old Scout Finch, To Kill a Mockingbird unfolds in Depression-era Alabama as her father, lawyer Atticus Finch, defends a Black man falsely accused of rape. The novel holds childhood wonder and adult injustice in the same frame.

Winner of the Pulitzer Prize in 1961, it remains one of the most widely taught novels in the English-speaking world and one of our most requested school-holiday reads.
